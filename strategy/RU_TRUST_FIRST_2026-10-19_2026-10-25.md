## Core positioning
**НЕ ВЕРЬ ОБЕЩАНИЯМ. ПРОВЕРЯЙ.**
We do not promise easy money, guaranteed incomes, fast results or effortless work. We show how to check real tasks, demand, learning offers and risks before spending time or money. Key voice: "Не верь мне. Проверь."
Do not present Sergey as a moral judge; show his own criteria and transparent verification.

## Audience and trust sequence
Skeptical beginners looking for online work; wary of polished promises, course scams, wasted money and information overload.
Progression: recognition of experience -> honest skepticism -> one practical verification -> sense of control -> earned trust -> voluntary profile interest.
Use vivid, humane and recognizable examples, not fearmongering or psychological coercion.

## Editorial rules
- One concrete problem, one specific takeaway, one clear CTA per item.
- No fantastical salary claims, guarantees, aggressive sales, shame or "change your life" slogans.
- Prefer checking actual work/tasks and demand before selecting or buying training.
- No generic AI talk for its own sake.
- Keep Reels focused on first 3 seconds and retention. Use metrics only where genuinely available.
- Manual publication by owner AFTER content approval. No automatic posting.
- Avoid «чек-лист» in audience-facing copy.
- Review Metricool at 2026-10-26; don't lock a monthly plan.
